Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Xerox Print Service, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and phone number when you create an account or sign in with Google.
  • Uploaded Files: Documents you upload for printing purposes. These are temporarily stored and automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • Print Preferences: Your selected print options such as color mode, paper size, copies, and binding.
  • Payment Information: Payment transactions are processed securely through Cashfree. We do not store your credit/debit card details.

2. Google Account Data

If you sign in with Google, we request access to:

  • Basic Profile: Your name and email address for account creation.
  • Google Drive (limited): We use the drive.file scope, which only allows our app to access files that it creates. We cannot see or access any other files in your Google Drive. Files uploaded through our service are stored in a dedicated "XeroxUploads" folder in your Drive.

You can revoke our access at any time through your Google Account permissions.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your print orders
  • To store your uploaded files temporarily for printing
  • To communicate order status and notifications
  • To process payments securely

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • Uploaded files are automatically deleted within 24 hours of upload.
  • Google Drive files remain in your own Google Drive under the "XeroxUploads" folder. You have full control to delete them at any time.
  • Account information is retained while your account is active.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Payment processing through Cashfree (subject to their privacy policy)
  • When required by law or legal process

6. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure token storage, and automatic file deletion.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Revoke Google Drive access at any time
  • Opt out of any communications
